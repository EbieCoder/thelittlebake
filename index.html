From Kitchen Experiments to Cape Town’s Favourite 100g Cookie

The Little Bake started in our Cape Town kitchen with a simple frustration: why were store-bought cookies either flat and crispy or overly sweet without real depth? We set out to engineer the ultimate New York-style cookie—tall, chunky, golden on the outside, and unapologetically gooey at the center.

Months of late-night test bakes, temperature trials, and ingredient tweaking went into perfecting our signature dough. Today, every single 100g cookie is weighed by hand, stuffed around a chilled cream cheese, caramel, or Belgian-style chocolate core, and cold-rested for at least 24 hours so the butter and brown sugars develop deep, toffee-like notes before hitting the oven.

“Seeing a customer take that first warm bite at the market—when the crisp edge gives way to a molten center—is the entire reason we bake.”

What began as boxes shared with family, friends, and neighbours in Tafelsig quickly grew into a weekly Sunday ritual under the oaks at Constantia’s Under the Tree Market (10:00 AM – 3:00 PM), and now delivers straight to homes and offices across Cape Town every Tuesday and Thursday.

Orders, Ingredients & Payments

Are your cookies made with sugar? Do you offer sugar-free options?
Yes—our cookies are traditional, indulgent artisanal treats baked with real white cane sugar and soft dark/light brown sugar. Brown sugar is essential to creating our signature chewy texture, caramelized edges, and deep toffee flavour. We do not currently offer sugar-free, diabetic-friendly, keto, gluten-free, or vegan cookies.
Is there a minimum or maximum order quantity?
  • No Minimum Order: You can order as little as a single 100g cookie for pickup or delivery.
  • Bundle Savings: Every 6-pack box saves R10 and every 12-pack box saves R30 compared to single-cookie prices.
  • Free Delivery Threshold: Cart subtotals of R300 or more automatically qualify for free Cape Town doorstep delivery.
  • Large / Corporate / Event Orders (24+ Cookies): For office platters, gifting, or events requiring 24 or more cookies, please place your order at least 48 hours prior to your required Tuesday or Thursday dispatch, or email orders@thelittlebake.co.za for custom scheduling.
What payment methods do you accept & is my payment secure?
All online checkout payments are processed securely via Paystack (a PCI-DSS Level 1 certified South African payment gateway). We accept:
  • Credit & Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express)
  • Apple Pay (1-tap checkout on iPhone, iPad & Mac)
  • SnapScan & Zapper QR
  • Instant EFT across all major South African banks
We never see or store your card details on our website. Your order enters our baking schedule the moment Paystack confirms payment and emails your official receipt.
What happens if my payment fails or disconnects?
If your browser closes or a bank OTP times out before Paystack confirms payment, no funds are deducted and the unpaid order will not enter our baking queue. If your bank app shows a deduction but you did not receive a confirmation email within 10 minutes, send your order reference or proof of payment to support@thelittlebake.co.za and we will verify and confirm your batch immediately.
Allergens & Dietary Information
All products contain wheat (gluten), dairy (real butter, milk, cream cheese), soy lecithin (in chocolate), and eggs. Our kitchen also handles peanuts and tree nuts (including coconut, almonds, and hazelnuts). Because all batches are prepared in the same artisanal facility, we cannot guarantee zero cross-contamination for severe allergies.
How should I store and reheat my cookies?
  • Room Temperature: Store in an airtight container for up to 4 days (keep cream cheese-stuffed flavours refrigerated if your kitchen is warm, then bring to room temperature or warm before eating).
  • Freezing: Individually wrap cookies in cling film and freeze in a zip-seal bag for up to 2 months.
  • The 12-Second Molten Trick: Microwave for 10–15 seconds (or bake at 160°C for 3 minutes) to restore crisp edges and an oozing molten core.
